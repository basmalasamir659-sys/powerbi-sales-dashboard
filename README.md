# Retail Sales & Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

📌 **Project Overview**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and regional profits. The report evaluates how product categories, discount strategies, and customer segments impact overall business revenue and profitability.

📊 **Key Insights & Metrics**

* **Total Revenue & Records:** Analyzed comprehensive sales data across multiple regions and product categories.
* **Performance Trends:** Identified peak sales periods, seasonal trends, and key revenue growth drivers.
* **Customer Insights:** Highlighted top-performing customer segments and product preferences to target future campaigns.
* **Profitability:** Evaluated profit margins and the impact of discounts across different categories to optimize pricing strategy.

🛠️ **Tools & Technologies Used**

* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, Star Schema design, advanced DAX measures, and interactive visual design.
* **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, merging tables, and conditional formatting.
* **Data Visualizations:** KPI Cards, Matrix tables, Line charts for trends, and Donut charts for distribution.
